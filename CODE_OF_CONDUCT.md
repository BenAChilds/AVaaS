# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in AVaaS harassment-free and welcoming. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the project. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
